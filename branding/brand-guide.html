01 — Positioning

Brand stance

Every visual decision, every word, every touchpoint runs through this filter. If it doesn't feel like tara, it doesn't ship.

"Your guiding star for code reviews — AI that reads the whole diff so you can focus on what matters."

Guiding Sharp Calm Local-first Trustworthy

We are

  • A calm, reliable review partner
  • AI that augments your judgment
  • Local-first, nothing leaves your machine
  • Open source, no vendor lock-in
  • Opinionated but editable

We are not

  • A replacement for human reviewers
  • A cloud service that reads your code
  • A linter or static analysis tool
  • Flashy, noisy, or attention-seeking
  • Enterprise-first or sales-driven
तारा

On the name

Tara (तारा) means star in Sanskrit. Like the Dhruv Tara — the Pole Star, the constant guiding light — code-tara is your guiding star for code reviews. The name carries warmth and personality that's rare in dev tools. It's not an acronym or a clever portmanteau. It's a name. That matters.

Usage rules

Minimum size

The star symbol should never appear smaller than 16×16px. At smaller sizes, use the compact "ct" mark or favicon variant.

Clear space

Maintain padding equal to the star's width on all sides. The mark needs room to breathe — it's a guiding star, not a crowded badge.

Don't do this

Never rotate the star. Never add glow or shadow effects. Never use the gold star on yellow or orange backgrounds. Never stretch or distort.

05 — Voice & tone

How tara speaks

Tara has a personality — calm, sharp, occasionally witty, never loud. The voice should feel like a senior engineer who writes thoughtful PR reviews, not a chatbot.

Direct, not blunt

Say what needs to be said. Don't pad with hedges. But be constructive, not dismissive.

✦ "This function does three things. Consider splitting it."
✕ "Maybe you could potentially think about splitting this?"

Calm confidence

No exclamation marks. No "exciting update!" energy. The tool works. That's enough.

✦ "tara reviewed your PR. 3 chunks, 12 comments."
✕ "🎉 tara has finished reviewing! Check out what we found!"

Technical precision

Use the right terms. Don't dumb down for marketing. The audience writes code.

✦ "Grouped into 4 chunks by logical dependency"
✕ "Smart AI organized your files into easy sections"

Lowercase default

The brand name is always "code-tara" or "tara" — never capitalized. Lowercase signals the tool's understated personality.

✦ "let tara review it"
✕ "Let Tara Review It"
